How to choose good makhana

Good makhana is easy to recognise once you know what to look for. Use this short checklist when you buy.

Check the makhana

Colour: creamy white. Small natural specks are common.

Shape: round, whole puffs of similar size, with few broken pieces.

Crunch: a good puff is light and snaps crisply when pressed, rather than feeling chewy or soft.

Smell: clean and neutral. A musty or damp smell is a bad sign.

Check the pack

Ingredient list should be only foxnuts (makhana).

Look for the FSSAI licence number, batch number, manufacturing and use-by dates, MRP and storage instructions.

Buy a sealed pack, and avoid packs that look damp or crushed.

Keep it crisp at home

Store in a cool, dry place away from sunlight, and close the pack tightly after opening. If the puffs soften, a 2 minute low-heat roast brings back the crunch.
