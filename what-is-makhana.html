What is makhana?

Makhana, also sold as fox nuts or foxnuts, is the puffed seed of the water plant Euryale ferox. It is a light, crunchy snack eaten across India, plain, roasted, in kheer and in chaat.

How makhana grows and is made

Makhana grows in still, shallow ponds, mainly in Bihar and nearby parts of eastern India. The seeds are collected from the pond bed, dried, sorted by size and then heated until they pop into white, round puffs.

Ways people enjoy it

  • Plain or lightly roasted with a pinch of salt
  • Masala makhana with chaat masala and pepper
  • Kheer cooked in milk
  • As a simple snack during vrat (fasting) in many households
