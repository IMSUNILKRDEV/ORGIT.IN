Simple makhana recipes

Makhana cooks in minutes and takes flavour well. These three recipes need very few ingredients.

Roasted makhana

Heat 1 teaspoon of ghee or oil in a pan on low heat. Add 2 cups of makhana.

Stir for 6 to 8 minutes until the puffs turn crisp and snap easily when pressed.

Add a pinch of salt and let it cool. Roast just before eating for the best crunch.

Masala makhana

Roast as above. While still warm, toss with chaat masala, a little black pepper and red chilli powder, or lime powder.

If it turns soft, re-roast for 2 minutes on low heat.

Makhana kheer

Lightly roast 1 cup of makhana and crush half of it. Boil 1 litre of milk.

Add the makhana and 2 crushed cardamoms and simmer for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until slightly thick.

Add sugar to taste. If using jaggery, add it after switching off the heat and letting the milk cool a little, so it does not curdle.

For vrat

Many families roast makhana in ghee with sendha namak (rock salt) during fasts. Follow your own family or local fasting rules.
